The “SPOMYN” Collection is a celebration of tradition and modernity, seamlessly blending the rich tapestry of Ukrainian culture with cutting-edge design. Yaryna Lahoda's vision is embodied in every stitch, showcasing a deep connection to her heritage while embracing sustainable and eco-friendly practices through the use of hemp fabric. Capturing the essence of this unique collection is the stunning model, Rita Markytan, from the renowned Look Models Int.
