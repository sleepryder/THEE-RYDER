The "OFFICE" is a bold reimagination of corporate chic, where professionalism meets allure and confidence takes center stage. Drawing inspiration from films like The Devil Wears Prada and Secretary, this photoshoot delves into the magnetic world of ambition, power dynamics, and sophistication. The contrasting atmosphere amplifies Nata Galagan's striking collection and styling by Yaryna Lahoda.
