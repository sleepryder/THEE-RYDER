Schiz. Drama

Type
Staged photography series
Artist
VVAIN
Location
Michalska Cocktail Room
Producer
VVAIN

The "Schiz. Drama" is a staged photography experiment, a journey into the depths of VVAIN's artistic psyche. The series shows his amazing change in a variety of settings, from a rustic western bar to the modern air of an office complex. Each shot constitutes a chapter in the dramatic narrative, capturing VVAIN's adaptability and transcendence. It's a visual symphony that reflects the breadth and complexity of his next album.
